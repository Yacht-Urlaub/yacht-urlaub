The route

A round trip from Koh Chang through the southern islands, usually 2 to 4 hours of sailing per day.

  1. Wed09 Dec
    Koh Chang MarinaCheck-in from 15:00. Settle into the cabin, shop for the first few days and enjoy a first dinner at the harbour.
  2. Thu10 Dec
    Koh WaiNo roads, no ATMs, barely any internet. Coral reefs off the north coast, snorkelling straight from the boat.
  3. Fri11 Dec
    Koh Mak · Ao KaoCoconut and banana plantations, explore the island by bike or scooter. Small beach bars on the south-west coast in the evening.
  4. Sat12 Dec
    Koh Kut · Ao TapaoFirst stop on the west coast of Koh Kut: a long sandy beach and clear water.
  5. Sun13 Dec
    Koh Kut · Ao Klong ChaoJungle and waterfalls. Kayak up the Klong Chao river to the waterfall.
  6. Mon14 Dec
    Koh Kut · Ao JarkQuiet swimming bays in the south of the island, dinner at anchor.
  7. Tue15 Dec
    Koh RangMarine national park and the archipelago’s best snorkelling and diving spot. A night off an uninhabited island.
  8. Wed16 Dec
    Koh KhamSmall island off Koh Mak with a sandy beach and reef, just a few nautical miles away.
  9. Thu17 Dec
    Koh Mak · Ao Suan Yai2 km of white sandy beach on the north-west coast, a viewpoint looking all the way to Koh Chang.
  10. Fri18 Dec
    Koh NgamSmall island group with a sandbank and reefs, ideal for a relaxed beach day.
  11. Sat19 Dec
    Bang Bao → MarinaStilted fishing village with a market and seafood restaurants. Back to the marina in the evening.
  12. Sun20 Dec
    Koh Chang MarinaCheck-out by 09:00.

The Fountaine Pajot Saona 47 'Maravelle' is the Quintet version with five double cabins, five heads and a skipper's forepeak with its own toilet. 4 double cabins are bookable for this trip. Plenty of space on deck with a cockpit, flybridge and trampoline, plus a shallow draft of just 1.30 m for the shallow bays of Phang Nga Bay.

Equipment
  • Air conditioning, generator and water maker
  • Electric toilets, bow thruster, autopilot
  • Dinghy with outboard and hydraulic lift, snorkelling gear
  • Flybridge, bimini, cockpit fridge, Bluetooth sound system

The route

A round trip from Phuket through Phang Nga Bay, along the Krabi coast to Koh Phi Phi and back via the Yao Islands: around 105 nautical miles in total, with an optional detour to James Bond Island (dashed). Usually 2 to 4 hours of sailing per day, with the longest leg (Phi Phi – Koh Rang Yai, approx. 25 nm) taking 5 to 6 hours.

  1. Tue02 Feb
    Ao Po Grand MarinaCheck-in from 15:00 at Ao Po Grand Marina on Phuket's north-east coast. Settle into your cabin, briefing by Philip, shopping for the first few days and a first dinner at the marina.
  2. Wed03 Feb
    Koh PhanakFirst leg into Phang Nga Bay with its limestone cliffs. Take the dinghy into the sea caves and hidden "hongs" (lagoons inside the rock) of Koh Phanak. Dinner on board, night at anchor.
  3. Thu04 Feb
    Koh RoiA short hop to the small, unspoilt island with a walk-in cave, sandy beach and coral for snorkelling. Early risers can take the detour to James Bond Island (Khao Phing Kan) and the stilted village of Koh Panyee before the tour boats arrive (an extra approx. 20 nm). Dinner under the stars.
  4. Fri05 Feb
    Koh Hong (Krabi)North around Koh Yao Noi, with a swim stop at Koh Pakbia. In the afternoon, take the dinghy through the narrow rock opening into the emerald-green lagoon of Koh Hong (tide-dependent). Night at anchor off the lagoon.
  5. Sat06 Feb
    RailayAlong the Krabi coast to Railay: West Railay with fine sand, world-famous climbing cliffs and sunset, a short walk to Phra Nang Beach. Dinner with your feet in the sand; Ao Nang is right next door for strolling.
  6. Sun07 Feb
    Koh Poda · Chicken IslandAn island day off Krabi: white beaches on Koh Poda, the Koh Tup sandbank and the "chicken head" rock of Chicken Island. Snorkelling in clear water, a quiet night at anchor.
  7. Mon08 Feb
    Koh Phi Phi DonAcross open water to Phi Phi. Tonsai Village with restaurants, bars and shops, a hike up to the Phi Phi Viewpoint, restock water and supplies. Fire shows on Loh Dalum Bay in the evening.
  8. Tue09 Feb
    Koh Phi Phi Leh · Monkey BeachAn early start before the tour boats arrive: dinghy into the Pileh Lagoon. Maya Bay can only be reached via the pontoon in Loh Samah Bay and a short jetty; swimming is not allowed there. Snorkelling at Monkey Beach in the afternoon, another night off Phi Phi Don.
  9. Wed10 Feb
    Koh Rang YaiThe longest leg, back towards Phuket, with a lunch and snorkelling stop at Koh Khai. Then on to Koh Rang Yai: palm-lined beaches and calm, clear water for swimming.
  10. Thu11 Feb
    Koh Yao NoiThe west coast of Koh Yao Noi: a rural island with rubber plantations and fishing villages, explore the island by scooter. Dinner at one of the quiet resorts on land.
  11. Fri12 Feb
    Koh Naka Yai → MarinaA last swim stop at Koh Naka Yai with a cocktail at the beach reggae bar, back to Ao Po Grand Marina in the afternoon. Farewell dinner on land.
  12. Sat13 Feb
    Ao Po Grand MarinaCheck-out by 09:00.
