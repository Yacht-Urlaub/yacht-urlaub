Why Porto Heli

The sailing area hardly anyone knows yet

Two hours from Athens, yet far from the bustle of the Cyclades. Anchor beneath climbing cliffs, eat in fish tavernas without a reservation and often have the bay to yourself.

Friday instead of Saturday

You cast off while others are still packing. One bridge day is enough, and by Saturday you are already in Nafplio.

Sheltered gulf, short hops

Daily legs of 11 to 25 nautical miles. Plenty of time for swimming, anchoring and exploring.

Places with history

Venetian Nafplio, the car-free rock town of Monemvasia, villages best reached from the water.

Catamarans built 2024–2026

Air conditioning, generator, watermaker and our skipper on board. Plus a welcome pack with Greek specialities.

Suggested route from Porto Heli · not for navigation

  1. FridayArrival

    Porto Heli

    Check-in from 5 pm, shopping and your first ouzo on the waterfront. Your skipper shows you the yacht, and you spend the first night in the marina.

  2. Saturdayapprox. 24 nm

    Nafplio

    Across the gulf to Greece's first capital. The Bourtzi water fortress, 999 steps up to the Palamidi fortress and, in the evening, tavernas in the Venetian lanes of the old town.

  3. Sundayapprox. 25 nm

    Leonidio (Plaka)

    South along the coast. Red climbing walls tower over the little harbour of Plaka, and the fish taverna sits right on the quay.

  4. Mondayapprox. 11 nm

    Kyparissi

    A short hop and a long day for swimming. The village could only be reached by boat for a long time and is still an insider tip with crystal-clear water.

  5. Tuesdayapprox. 20 nm

    Gerakas & MonemvasiaHighlight

    Lunch break in the fjord of Gerakas, then the highlight of the week in the evening: the car-free rock town of Monemvasia with Byzantine lanes and views to the horizon.

  6. Wednesdayapprox. 20 nm

    Kyparissi

    Up to the upper town in the morning, then back north with a swim stop in a bay beneath the Parnon mountains.

  7. Thursdayapprox. 25 nm

    Spetses & Porto Heli

    Lunch stop on the island of Spetses with its captains’ houses and horse-drawn carriages, then back to base in the evening.

  8. FridayDeparture

    Porto Heli

    Check-out by 9 am. Breakfast on board, then back to Athens by ferry or transfer.

Extension tip

Elafonisos & Kythira

Beyond Cape Maleas you will find Simos beach on Elafonisos and the island of Kythira. Doable without rushing in 10 to 14 nights, and we are happy to plan it with you.

Via Poros, Hydra and Spetses to Porto Heli, approx. 2 h 40 min. The journey itself is already an excursion across the Saronic Gulf.

From the airport to Piraeus by metro or taxi. Check the 2027 timetable before booking.

Straight from Athens airport to the yacht, up to 8 people, approx. 2.5 hours. The most comfortable option for groups with luggage.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a sailing licence?

No. Our skipper is always on board on this cruise and takes responsibility for the yacht. Joining in on the sailing is very welcome.

Is the booking binding straight away?

Your booking request is binding. We check availability and confirm the booking to you within 24 hours. The contract only comes into being with our booking confirmation. If the week is no longer available, we will let you know and, if you wish, offer you an alternative. You pay nothing as long as no booking has been confirmed.

What is included in the price?

One price for everything around the yacht: catamaran for 7 nights, Yacht-Urlaub skipper, all charter extras, dinghy with outboard, bed linen, towels and welcome pack. On site you only pay the kitty (fuel, harbour fees, provisions incl. the skipper) and leave the deposit.

Where does the skipper sleep?

In the crew cabin. The guest cabins stay yours, which is why we state the number of guests without skipper for each yacht.

How windy is it in the Argolic Gulf?

The gulf is well sheltered and usually calmer than the Cyclades. In high summer a fresh breeze often blows in the afternoon, ideal for sailing.

How do I pay?

Payment is made in two instalments: 50% on booking, with the booking confirmation that you receive from us shortly after your booking request, and the remaining 50% six weeks before the start of your trip. Nothing is paid when you book on this page.
