FAQ

Frequently asked questions about our cruises

Do I need sailing experience for a cruise?

No, no prior sailing experience is required. If you like, you can take the helm and trim the sails yourself at any time – but there is no obligation to do so.

Do I need a boating licence?

No. A boating licence isn't required for our skippered sailing cruises – the skipper handles all navigation and responsibility on board.

Who is on board with us?

On board are the travellers listed in your booking plus the skipper, who is responsible for your safety and the wellbeing of all guests and is available to you around the clock.

Can children come along?

Yes, children are very welcome. There is no fixed minimum age – it's up to you as parents to judge what suits your child. If you have questions about the right route or travel time for families, we're happy to advise you.

How do I book a cruise?

You can either book a cruise package directly online, or use the holiday planner or the enquiry form for an individual cruise. After an enquiry you receive an offer and can reserve your preferred yacht without obligation for seven days (option).
