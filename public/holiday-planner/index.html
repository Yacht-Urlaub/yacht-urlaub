The most common type with by far the widest choice – and also the most affordable.

Frequently asked questions about the holiday planner

How much does the holiday planner cost?

Nothing: the holiday planner is free and non-binding. After your enquiry you receive an offer and can reserve your preferred yacht without obligation for seven days (option).

How long does the holiday planner take?

Just 5 short steps: choice of yacht, sailing area, schedule, crew & communication and your contact details. After that we get in touch with you personally.

Do I need sailing experience or a boating licence?

No. No prior sailing experience is required, and a boating licence isn't needed for our skippered sailing trips – the skipper handles all navigation and responsibility on board.

What is the difference between a sailing yacht and a motor yacht?

Both types are motorised. Motor yachts offer more space and more powerful engines (speeds of up to 40 knots and more). Sailing yachts are a little quieter underway, more affordable and use wind power.
